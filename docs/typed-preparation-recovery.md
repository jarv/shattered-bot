# Typed blockers and bounded sentry reobservation

`BlockerKind` distinguishes optional work, navigation stalls, unresolved
mandatory preparation, immediate safety emergencies, execution failures and
unclassified failures. Exceptions carry the type through tactical/until-death
recovery. Human-readable wording no longer admits floor abandonment.
Unclassified sources fail closed; explicit producer updates can expand coverage
without inventing permissive text patterns.

The survival runner records every terminal blockage, including a plain
`Finished("blocked", ...)` safety stop. Both the private outcome and incident
contain its category; run metadata exposes the terminal `blocker_kind`. Safety
stops remain safety stops, not attempts to descend through gas or ranged fire.

The existing committed-stash-bank correction and duration-checked gas-return
detour are retained. A reconnaissance trip is another separate transaction:
`preparation_scout` owns its target until accepted arrival, counts accepted
inputs (including opening a door without moving), and visits at most four
vantages within 48 inputs and an eight-input nonprogress bound.

For an incompletely observed red-sentry enclosure, the new prerequisite
considers only observed ordinary ground and ordinary door thresholds with
potential sight to missing perimeter cells. The pinned game's
`SentryRoom.Sentry.act` targets `Terrain.EMPTY_SP` (14); reconnaissance never
walks that terrain, traps, effects, plants, locks, unknown cells, occupied or
blocked squares, or protected reverse edges. Hunger, HP, threats and active
retrieval checks remain in force. Reaching a viewpoint creates no missing-wall,
potion-identity, protection, collection or completed-room receipt.

The provider runs after ordinary room preparation and known reveal resources
decline, before mandatory discovery declares its prerequisites exhausted.
Each accepted movement is followed by real observations and a fresh attempt by
the existing room geometry, storage and protection controllers.
