# Vertigo and converging enemies

## Vertigo (run 54)

Confusion gas renews vertigo: waiting in its source is not recovery. The
high-priority handler prefers known protection or suitable transport, otherwise
admits a single movement input only when all eight random directions are
observed and harmless. Known walls may cause a stationary bump; unknown cells,
chasms, doors, active traps, plants and damaging effects are not certified safe.
Harmless confusion gas is allowed on the escape route.

Reobserve after every accepted input. A requested destination is not proof of
movement or gas escape. Count wall bumps and unexpected landings against the
12-input movement budget. Outside exposure, wait at most 12 inputs for residual
vertigo; two observed nondecreasing durations force reassessment. Starvation,
low health, threats and mixed damaging effects prohibit a calm waiting plan.
No admissible recovery ends explicitly as `stuck-planner`, not starvation.

Preserve reserved puzzle supplies when a free escape exists. Last-resort purity
use carries an explicit emergency purpose; shared consumption receipts account
for spent supplies without awarding room completion or collection.

## Converging enemies (run 56)

Do not wait for two enemies to be adjacent. Multiple near, awake enemies or
multiple possible attack approach slots trigger retreat before optional
preparation. Emergency healing/control retains its existing priority.

Prefer the nearest admissible observed door, measured by safe route length.
Recheck the route after each input, including enemy exposure, health loss and
the accepted-input budget. Opening or reaching a door does not prove safety:
only observed, flight-aware single-file geometry permits a far-side hold.
Keep an already verified single-file position rather than fleeing needlessly.
If no usable door exists, retain the existing single-attacker-square fallback
and best-effort combat. No retreat is a promise of survival.

Regression fixtures use reduced public observations from runs 54 and 56 and
synthetic outcomes. They do not replay active games or establish survival gains.
